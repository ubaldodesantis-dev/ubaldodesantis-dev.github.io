Ubaldo Electric MX Bot

Ubaldo Electric MX Bot is a personal tool used only by Ubaldo De Santis to publish his own electric motocross videos to his own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

The app connects only to the owner's accounts through the official APIs (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API, YouTube Data API, Instagram Graph API). It does not offer accounts to other people and does not collect data from other users.
