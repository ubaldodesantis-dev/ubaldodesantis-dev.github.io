Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Ubaldo Electric MX Bot ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Ubaldo De Santis to upload and publish videos to his own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

Data we access

When the owner authorizes the app, it receives from TikTok the basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and access tokens needed to upload and publish videos (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish). On YouTube and Instagram it accesses only the owner's channel and account.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish the owner's own videos and to check the posting settings of his account. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app does not access data of any other user.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own server and are deleted when access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions) or from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Third-party terms

Use of the platforms is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Meta's terms.
